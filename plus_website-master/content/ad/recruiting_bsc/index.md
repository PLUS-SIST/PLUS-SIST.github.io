We provide a list of background materials for the undergraduate students who are interested in our research projects:

- **Machine Learning**: [Andrew Ng's course](https://www.coursera.org/learn/machine-learning) ([video](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV164411b7dx/)) or [Hung-yi Lee's course](http://speech.ee.ntu.edu.tw/~tlkagk/courses_ML20.html) ([video](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1JE411g7XF/))  
- **Deep Learning and Pytorch**: [Dive into Deep Learning](https://github.com/dsgiitr/d2l-pytorch) or [Stanford CS231n](http://cs231n.stanford.edu/syllabus.html)
- **Computer Vision**: [UPenn Course](https://www.edx.org/course/robotics-vision-intelligence-and-machine-learning) or [GaTech Course](https://www.udacity.com/course/introduction-to-computer-vision--ud810)

We also recommend the following *reference* books on machine learning and computer vision:

- **PRML**: [website](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/cmbishop/prml-book/)
- **MLAPA**: [website](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~murphyk/MLbook/)
- **CVAA**: [website](http://szeliski.org/Book/)
- **CVMLI**: [website](http://www.computervisionmodels.com/)
